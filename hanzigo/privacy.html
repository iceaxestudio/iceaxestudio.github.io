Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Information we use

You may use free lessons without an account. If you create an account, HanziGo uses your email address, authentication information, learning progress, earned titles and Premium access status to provide account features and restore access across devices.

Payments

Android purchases are processed by Google Play. HanziGo receives purchase identifiers and subscription status needed to verify and restore Premium access. HanziGo does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Storage and sharing

Account and access data is stored using Supabase. Purchase status is verified with Google Play. We do not sell personal information and the app does not contain advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account from Account settings in the app. Deletion removes the HanziGo account and associated cloud data; Google Play purchase records remain subject to Google’s own retention requirements.

Children

HanziGo is a general-audience language-learning app and is not directed specifically to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.

Contact

For privacy or account-deletion questions, use the support email published on HanziGo’s Google Play store listing.
