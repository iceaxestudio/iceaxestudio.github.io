Delete your HanziGo account

You can permanently delete your account inside HanziGo or request deletion by email if you cannot access the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open HanziGo and tap Account.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open Account settings and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion when prompted.

Request deletion without the app

Email support.iceaxestudio@gmail.com from the email address linked to your HanziGo account. Use the subject HanziGo account deletion request. We may need to verify account ownership before processing your request. Never send your password.

Data removed with your account

  • Your HanziGo authentication account and email association
  • Cloud-saved learning progress associated with your account
  • Your HanziGo Premium entitlement and purchase-to-account association

Progress or preferences stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's storage.

Google Play purchases

Google Play may retain transaction records under its own legal and accounting requirements. HanziGo does not store your complete payment-card details.

Deleting your HanziGo account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play before deleting your account.
