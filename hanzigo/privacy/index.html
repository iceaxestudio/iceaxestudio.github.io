Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Information we use

You can use free lessons without an account. If you create an account, HanziGo uses your email address, authentication information, learning progress, earned titles, and Premium access status to provide account features and restore access across devices. Some learning preferences and progress are also stored on your device.

Purchases

Google Play processes Android purchases. HanziGo receives purchase identifiers and subscription status needed to verify and restore Premium access. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

Storage and sharing

Supabase stores account and access data. Google Play verifies purchases. We use these services to operate HanziGo. We do not sell personal information, and the app does not contain advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account from Account settings in the app or request deletion by email. Deletion removes your HanziGo account and associated cloud data. Google Play may retain purchase records under its own requirements. Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app storage.

Children

HanziGo is a general-audience language-learning app and is not directed specifically to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.
