Delete your PriceBook account

Request permanent deletion of your PriceBook account and its associated cloud data. You can make this request without installing or signing in to the app.

Request deletion

  1. Email support.iceaxestudio@gmail.com from the email address registered to your PriceBook account.
  2. Use the subject PriceBook account deletion request and state that you want your PriceBook account and associated cloud data deleted.
  3. We may reply to confirm ownership. After verification, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.
Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send an email manually using the address above. Never send your password, payment-card details, or API keys. If you cannot access your registered email, contact support for help verifying ownership.

What we delete

  • Your PriceBook sign-in account and associated email address.
  • Your private cloud backup, including backed-up purchases, price records, products, income entries, dishes, meal plans, basket items, and preferences.
  • Account-linked Plus entitlement and purchase associations held by PriceBook.
  • Community price reports associated with your PriceBook account.

This request applies only to PriceBook. Accounts in HanziGo or other IceAxe Studio apps are unaffected.

Device data and subscriptions

Account deletion does not remotely erase records or photos stored only on your device. To remove those, clear PriceBook's app storage in Android Settings. Also remove any photos or exported files you saved separately.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play to stop future renewals. You can contact support for deletion without an active subscription.

Retention

The active PriceBook account data listed above is deleted when the verified request is completed; no additional retention period is applied to that active data. We retain the deletion-request correspondence for up to 90 days after completion to resolve questions about the request, then delete it unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

Google Play transaction records and advertising-provider records are controlled by those providers and subject to their own retention policies. Account deletion does not erase their independently held records.
