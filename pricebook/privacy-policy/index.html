Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

PriceBook is provided by IceAxe Studio. Questions or privacy requests: support.iceaxestudio@gmail.com.

Information PriceBook handles

When you create or use an account, PriceBook handles your email address and account ID. Information you enter may include products, prices, purchase records, income entries, dishes, meal plans, basket items, and preferences.

Your records may be stored on your device. If you use account backup or restore, relevant records are sent to Supabase, our cloud service provider. Photos are processed on your device and are not included in cloud backups.

If you choose to submit a community price report, the report is sent to our service and may be shown to other users as community price information.

Advertising and device information

PriceBook uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Its SDK may collect and share IP address information (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure. Manage ad personalization at Google My Ad Center.

How information is used and shared

We use information to provide PriceBook features, sign-in, backup and restore, community price reports, support, and app security. We do not sell personal information.

Supabase provides cloud services, and Google provides advertising services. A community price report you choose to submit may be shared with other users. Google Play processes payments made through Google Play; PriceBook does not receive your full payment-card number.

Security and retention

Data sent to online services is protected in transit using encrypted connections. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Account-linked cloud data is kept while your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

Your choices and deletion

You choose whether to use cloud backup and whether to submit community price reports. To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, visit our PriceBook account deletion page. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Deleting your account does not erase information stored only on your device.

Children and policy changes

PriceBook is intended for adults and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us. We may update this policy when our app or data practices change; the revised date will appear at the top of this page.
